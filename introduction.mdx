HiiFest is an all-in-one event management platform built for Indonesia's fast-growing events industry. Whether you're launching a music festival, corporate conference, or intimate workshop, HiiFest gives you every tool you need — from ticket sales and attendee management to vendor sourcing and real-time analytics — under a single, unified platform.

## What is HiiFest?

HiiFest brings together the entire event ecosystem in one place. Organizers create and sell tickets quickly and easily. Attendees discover, buy, and manage their event experiences seamlessly. Vendors and venue managers showcase their services and spaces to a ready audience of event professionals.

The platform delivers **real-time analytics** so you always know how your event is performing. Secure payment processing means you spend less time on logistics and more time building memorable experiences.

## Key Features

- **Fast event creation** — go from idea to a live event page in just a few steps
- **Custom ticket tiers** — create Early Bird, VIP, General Admission, and fully customizable ticket types
- **Real-time analytics dashboard** — monitor ticket sales, revenue, and attendee demographics live
- **Vendor directory** — source and coordinate with service providers from a curated marketplace
- **Venue listings** — browse and book spaces directly through the platform
- **Team member management** — invite and assign roles to your event team
- **Secure payment processing** — competitive transaction fees based on your plan
- **Multi-workspace support** — operate as an organizer, vendor, or venue manager under a single account
