Your HiiFest account is the foundation for everything you do on the platform — whether you're organizing events, buying tickets, listing services, or managing a venue. This guide walks you through creating your account, selecting the right workspace type, and keeping your settings in good shape.

## Creating your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the registration page">
    Go to [platform.hiifest.app/auth/register](https://platform.hiifest.app/auth/register) in your browser.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign up with Google or LinkedIn">
    Click the **Continue with Google** or **Continue with LinkedIn** button. HiiFest pulls your name and email address from the selected provider — no password required. You're redirected to workspace selection after authorizing the connection.

    <Info>
      Signing up with Google or LinkedIn lets you log in with a single click on future visits. You can always add a password later from your security settings.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Or sign up with email and password">
    If you prefer to use an email address directly:

    1. Enter your **full name**.
    2. Enter your **email address**.
    3. Create a **password** (use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols for best security).
    4. Click **Create account**.

    HiiFest sends a verification email to your inbox. Open it and click **Verify my account** to activate your profile before you can log in.

    <Warning>
      If you don't see the verification email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. You can request a new verification link from the login page.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing your workspace type

When you log in for the first time, HiiFest asks you to choose a workspace type. Your selection determines which features and dashboards are available to you.

| Workspace type | Who it's for | Key capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| **Join events** | Attendees | Browse events, purchase tickets, manage bookings |
| **Create events** | Organizers | Build event pages, sell tickets, manage attendees, access analytics |
| **Offer services** | Vendors | List service packages, receive organizer inquiries, collaborate on events |
| **List venue** | Venue managers | Showcase spaces, manage availability, handle booking requests |

Click the tile that best matches your primary use case, then click **Continue**.

<Note>
  You are not locked into a single workspace type. You can add additional workspaces — for example, acting as both an organizer and a vendor — from your account settings after completing initial setup.
</Note>

## Account profile settings

After onboarding, customize your profile so others on the platform can recognize and trust you.

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner of any page, then select **Profile settings**.
2. Update the following fields:

   - **Display name** — the name shown publicly on event pages and directory listings.
   - **Profile photo or logo** — upload a square image, at least 200 × 200 px.
   - **Bio** — write a short description of yourself or your organization (max 300 characters).
   - **Website and social links** — add your website, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, or other relevant links.
   - **Phone number** — recommended for organizers and vendors to stay reachable for event-related inquiries.

3. Click **Save changes**.

<Tip>
  A complete profile with a logo and bio builds credibility with attendees and organizers. Profiles with photos receive significantly more engagement on the platform.
</Tip>

## Security settings

Keep your account secure by regularly reviewing your authentication methods and active sessions.

### Password management

1. Navigate to **Settings → Security**.
2. Under **Password**, click **Change password**.
3. Enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password.
4. Click **Update password**.

<Info>
  If you signed up with Google or LinkedIn and have never set a password, click **Set a password** instead. This lets you log in with email and password as an alternative method.
</Info>

### Connected accounts

From **Settings → Security → Connected accounts**, you can:

- **Link** a Google or LinkedIn account to enable one-click login.
- **Unlink** a connected provider, provided you have at least one other login method (password or alternate provider) active on your account.

### Active sessions

Under **Settings → Security → Sessions**, you see a list of devices and browsers currently logged in to your account. Click **Revoke** next to any session you don't recognize to log it out immediately.

<Warning>
  If you see an unrecognized session, revoke it immediately and change your password. Contact HiiFest support at [support@hiifest.id](mailto:support@hiifest.id) if you suspect unauthorized access.
</Warning>

## Switching between workspaces

If you manage multiple roles on the platform — for example, you organize your own events and also list venue spaces — you can operate multiple workspaces under a single HiiFest account.

**To add a new workspace:**

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Add workspace** from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the workspace type you want to add (**Create events**, **Offer services**, or **List venue**).
4. Complete the short setup flow for that workspace type.

**To switch between workspaces:**

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner.
2. Under **My workspaces**, select the workspace you want to switch to.

Your dashboard, settings, and features update instantly to reflect the selected workspace.

<Note>
  Each workspace has its own profile, analytics, and billing settings. Switching workspaces does not affect the others.
</Note>
