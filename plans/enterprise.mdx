## Security and Compliance

Enterprise is designed for organizations with strict security, identity, and audit requirements.

- **Single Sign-On (SSO):** Connect HiiFest to your existing identity provider so team members log in once and access the platform without a separate credential.
- **SCIM provisioning:** Automate the addition and removal of users as your team changes. No manual account management at scale.
- **SLA guarantees:** Your contract includes explicit uptime commitments and escalation procedures for critical incidents.
- **Custom limits:** Every scale parameter and feature access level is configured specifically for your organization's requirements as part of your contract.

## Custom Pricing and Contracts

Enterprise pricing is not published as a fixed rate because every engagement is scoped individually. Your contract covers:

- **Negotiated transaction fee** — Work with the HiiFest team to agree a rate that reflects your event volume and revenue.
- **Custom billing model** — Annual, multi-year, or milestone-based billing structures are all available.
- **Custom limits** — Set the exact scale parameters and feature access appropriate for your organization.
- **Dedicated support team** — A named team of support specialists is assigned to your account, with direct escalation paths and defined response SLAs.

<Note>
  There is no self-serve signup for the Enterprise plan. All Enterprise engagements start with a discovery call to scope your requirements before any contract is issued.
</Note>
