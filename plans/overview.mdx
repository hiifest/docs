## Billing: Monthly or Annual

Every paid plan is available on a **monthly** or **annual** billing cycle. Paying annually saves you up to **20%** compared to the monthly rate — ideal if you run events year-round and want predictable costs.

- **Monthly billing** — Full flexibility. Cancel or change plans at the end of any billing cycle.
- **Annual billing** — Pay once for the year and unlock the discounted rate. You can still switch plans as your needs evolve.

There are no long-term commitments on any plan. Cancel anytime.

## Transaction Fees

HiiFest charges a per-ticket transaction fee that decreases as you move up plans:

| Plan       | Transaction Fee |
|------------|-----------------|
| Starter    | 4.0%            |
| Growth     | 2.5%            |
| Pro        | 1.5%            |
| Enterprise | Negotiated      |

For high-revenue events, upgrading your plan can more than offset the plan cost through fee savings. See the full breakdown — including a worked revenue example — on the [Compare Plans](/plans/compare) page.

<Tip>
  **Growth first-month offer:** Eligible new organizers can access Growth with full features and no payment required today. Your subscription starts at Rp 1.490.000/month after the first month — cancel before then and you won't be charged.
</Tip>
