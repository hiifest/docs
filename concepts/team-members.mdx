Running an event is rarely a solo effort. HiiFest lets you bring your entire operations team into your organizer workspace so everyone can collaborate in one place. Team members can help with event creation, attendee management, ticket operations, and more — each with access controlled by the role you assign. The number of team members you can invite depends on your current plan.

## Team member limits by plan

Each HiiFest organizer plan includes a maximum number of team member seats. Review your plan's limit before inviting your team.

| Plan | Team Member Limit | Monthly Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 2 team members | Rp 499.000 / month |
| **Growth** | 5 team members | Rp 1.490.000 / month |
| **Pro** | 15 team members | Rp 3.990.000 / month |
| **Enterprise** | Unlimited | Custom pricing |

<Tip>
  If you're approaching your team member limit, upgrade your plan from **Workspace Settings → Billing**. Your existing team members and their roles carry over automatically after an upgrade.
</Tip>

## How to invite a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Workspace Settings">
    In your Organizer workspace, click **Settings** in the left sidebar, then select the **Team** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Invite Team Member'">
    Select the **Invite Team Member** button in the top-right corner of the Team page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the invitee's email address">
    Type the email address of the person you want to add. They must have a registered HiiFest account or will be prompted to create one when they accept the invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a role">
    Choose a role from the dropdown to define what this team member can see and do in your workspace. Review the role descriptions below before confirming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. Your invitee receives an email with a link to accept the invitation. The invitation expires after 7 days — resend it from the Team tab if needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A pending invitation counts toward your team member seat limit. If an invite expires without being accepted, the seat becomes available again automatically.
</Note>

## Team member roles

Assign the right role to each team member to keep your workspace secure and your workflows clean. HiiFest provides the following roles for organizer workspaces:

| Role | What They Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full access to the workspace — create and edit events, manage attendees, view analytics, manage team members, and access billing. Equivalent to the workspace owner except they cannot delete the workspace. |
| **Event Manager** | Create, edit, publish, and manage events and ticket tiers. View attendee lists and check-in data. Cannot access billing or team settings. |
| **Marketing** | Access event pages and analytics. Cannot create events or view attendee personal data. |
| **Check-in Staff** | Scan and validate attendee QR codes at in-person events. View the attendee check-in list for assigned events only. Read-only access — cannot modify any event data. |
| **Finance** | View transaction reports, payout summaries, and ticket sales data. Cannot edit events or access attendee contact information. |

<Warning>
  Only assign the **Admin** role to team members you fully trust with your workspace. Admins can invite additional team members, modify events, and access sensitive attendee data.
</Warning>

## Removing a team member

Remove a team member at any time if their involvement in your workspace ends. Removing a team member immediately revokes their access to all events, data, and tools in your organizer workspace.

To remove a team member:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Team tab">
    Go to **Settings → Team** in your organizer workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the team member">
    Find the team member you want to remove in the list of active members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select 'Remove'">
    Click the **⋯** menu next to their name and choose **Remove from Workspace**. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Removing a team member does not delete their HiiFest account. They simply lose access to your workspace. Any event changes or attendee records they created remain intact and are not affected by their removal.
</Note>
