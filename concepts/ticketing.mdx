HiiFest provides a fully integrated ticketing system that handles everything from ticket creation to secure payment processing and instant payouts. As an organizer, you define your ticket tiers and prices. As an attendee, you move from discovery to confirmation in just a few taps. All transactions flow through HiiFest's secure payment infrastructure, with fees automatically deducted from organizer payouts based on your plan.

## Ticket tiers

As an organizer, you can create multiple ticket tiers for a single event. Each tier has its own name, price, quantity, and optional description. Common tier types include:

| Tier Type | Typical Use |
|---|---|
| **Early Bird** | Discounted tickets for the first wave of buyers. Set a quantity or expiry date to create urgency. |
| **General Admission** | Your standard ticket tier, available for the majority of your audience. |
| **VIP** | Premium access with added perks — front-row seating, backstage access, exclusive lounges, etc. |
| **Custom** | Name your tier anything that fits your event — Student, Group, Press, Sponsor, and so on. |

<Tip>
  Use multiple tiers strategically. An Early Bird tier rewards your most loyal audience and drives early momentum, which increases the chance of your event being featured on HiiFest.
</Tip>

## Pricing

Set the price for each ticket tier in **Indonesian Rupiah (IDR)**. You control the face value — HiiFest deducts its transaction fee from your payout, so the price your attendees see is the price they pay.

You can also create **free tiers** for events that require registration but no payment, such as community meetups or sponsored workshops.

## Transaction fees

HiiFest's transaction fee depends on your organizer plan. The fee is automatically deducted from each payout — attendees are never charged extra.

| Plan | Transaction Fee | Monthly Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 4% per transaction | Rp 499.000 / month |
| **Growth** | 2.5% per transaction | Rp 1.490.000 / month |
| **Pro** | 1.5% per transaction | Rp 3.990.000 / month |
| **Enterprise** | Negotiated rate | Custom pricing |

<Info>
  Transaction fees are deducted from your organizer payouts, not added on top of the ticket price. The price you set is exactly what your attendees pay at checkout.
</Info>

## Attendee purchase flow

Purchasing a ticket on HiiFest is designed to be fast and frictionless.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover the event">
    Browse events by city or category, search by name, or follow a shared link. Open the event page to see all available ticket tiers, event details, and the venue or stream information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a ticket tier">
    Choose the tier that suits you — Early Bird, General, VIP, or any custom tier the organizer has created. Select your quantity and review what's included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check out securely">
    Enter your details and complete payment through HiiFest's secure checkout. HiiFest supports major Indonesian payment methods including bank transfer, credit/debit card, and e-wallets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your confirmation">
    Immediately after payment, you receive a booking confirmation by email and in your HiiFest dashboard. Your ticket includes a unique QR code for check-in at in-person events or a stream link for virtual events.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ticket management for attendees

Once you purchase a ticket, you manage it entirely from your **Attendee dashboard**.

- **Access your tickets** — Navigate to **My Tickets** in your dashboard to view all active and past tickets, download them as PDFs, or display the QR code directly from your phone.
- **Transfer a ticket** — You can transfer a ticket to another HiiFest user before the event starts. Open the ticket, select **Transfer**, and enter the recipient's registered email address.
- **Cancellations** — Cancellation eligibility depends on the organizer's refund policy, which is displayed on the event page before purchase. Review the policy carefully before completing checkout.

<Warning>
  Ticket transfers and cancellations must be completed before the event's check-in window opens. After check-in begins, tickets are non-transferable and refunds are subject to the organizer's discretion.
</Warning>
