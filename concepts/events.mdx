An **event** on HiiFest is the core unit of the platform. It represents any organized gathering — physical, virtual, or both — that an organizer creates, publishes, and sells tickets for. Every event has a format, a category, a status, and a set of ticket tiers. Understanding how these pieces fit together helps you create events that are easy to discover and seamless to attend.

## Event formats

HiiFest supports three event formats to accommodate the full range of how modern events are run.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="In-Person" icon="location-dot">
    Held at a physical venue in a specific city. Attendees check in on-site and the event page displays your venue address and directions. Works with HiiFest's venue booking directory so organizers can connect with venue managers directly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Virtual" icon="display">
    Streamed online with no physical venue required. Ideal for webinars, online workshops, and remote conferences. Attendees receive a link upon ticket confirmation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hybrid" icon="globe">
    Combines a physical venue with a live online stream. Lets you serve both in-person and remote audiences under one event, with separate ticket tiers for each experience.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Event categories

When you create an event, you assign it one category. Categories power search and discovery — attendees filter events by category to find what interests them. HiiFest currently supports the following categories:

- 🎵 Music
- 💻 Technology
- 🛠️ Workshop
- 🤝 Networking
- 🎨 Arts & Culture
- 🧘 Wellness
- ✨ Lifestyle
- 🏅 Sports
- 😄 Comedy
- 💼 Business
- 📚 Education

<Tip>
  Choose the category that most accurately describes your event's primary focus. A well-matched category improves your event's visibility in filtered search results and increases the likelihood of being featured on the HiiFest homepage for that category.
</Tip>

## Event status lifecycle

Every event moves through four statuses from creation to completion. Understanding each stage helps you plan your publishing and promotion timeline.

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Your event is saved but not visible to the public. Use this stage to build out your event details, upload assets, and configure ticket tiers before going live. |
| **Published** | Your event is publicly visible and ticket sales are open. Attendees can discover, share, and purchase tickets. |
| **Live** | Your event is actively happening. Check-ins are enabled for in-person events, and the virtual stream link is active for online attendees. |
| **Completed** | Your event has ended. Ticket sales are closed. Analytics, attendee records, and payout summaries remain accessible in your dashboard indefinitely. |

<Note>
  You can move an event back to **Draft** from **Published** at any time before ticket sales begin. Once at least one ticket has been sold, unpublishing requires you to contact HiiFest support to protect attendees.
</Note>

## Event discovery

Attendees find your event through several discovery surfaces on HiiFest:

- **City filter** — Attendees select their city (Jakarta, Bali, Surabaya, or Bandung) to see events near them. Make sure your event's location is set accurately.
- **Category filter** — Attendees browse by interest category. Your assigned category determines where your event appears in these filtered views.
- **Featured events** — HiiFest editors curate a featured section on the homepage and category pages. High-quality event pages with complete details, compelling cover images, and strong early ticket sales are more likely to be featured.
- **Search** — Attendees can search by event name, organizer, or keyword. Keep your event title and description clear and specific.
