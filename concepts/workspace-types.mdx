Every account on HiiFest is built around **workspaces**. A workspace defines your role on the platform and determines which tools, dashboards, and features you can access. Because event ecosystems involve many different participants, HiiFest gives you four distinct workspace types — each purpose-built for a specific way of engaging with events.

## Workspace Types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Attendee" icon="ticket">
    Browse events across Indonesia, purchase tickets, and manage all your bookings from one dashboard. Keep track of upcoming and past events in a single, organised view.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Organizer" icon="calendar-plus">
    Create and publish events, sell tickets across multiple tiers, manage your attendee list, and track performance with a real-time analytics dashboard. Invite your team and assign roles to collaborate across your workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Vendor" icon="store">
    List your event services and packages — from catering to AV equipment to photography — and make them discoverable to organizers across the platform. Receive and respond to inquiries directly through your vendor workspace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Venue Manager" icon="building">
    List your spaces, set availability, define capacity and layout options, and receive booking inquiries from event organizers. Keep your availability calendar up to date so organizers can plan with confidence.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Switching and Adding Workspaces

You are not limited to a single workspace type. You can add additional workspaces to your account at any time — for example, you might run an Organizer workspace for your events while also maintaining a Vendor workspace to offer production services to other organizers.

To add a new workspace:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account dashboard">
    Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner of the HiiFest dashboard to open the account menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select 'Add Workspace'">
    Choose **Add Workspace** from the dropdown. You will see the four available workspace types listed with a brief description of each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your workspace type">
    Select the workspace type you want to create and follow the setup prompts. Each type has its own short onboarding flow to configure your profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch between workspaces">
    Once created, switch between your workspaces at any time using the workspace switcher in the top navigation bar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can hold multiple workspace types under a single HiiFest account. All workspaces share the same login credentials and billing profile, so you never need to manage separate accounts.
</Note>
