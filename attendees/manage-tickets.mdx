All your HiiFest tickets live in one place: your attendee dashboard. From there you can view upcoming event details, download your QR code for offline access, transfer a ticket to a friend, and review the cancellation terms set by the event organiser.

## Access Your Tickets

1. Log in to your account at [platform.hiifest.app](https://platform.hiifest.app).
2. Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner and select **Dashboard**.
3. Navigate to the **Tickets** tab in the left sidebar.

You'll see a list of all your purchased tickets, organised by upcoming and past events.

## View Ticket Details

Click on any ticket to open the full ticket detail view. Each ticket displays:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Event Information" icon="calendar">
    Event name, date, time, and venue address or virtual event link.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ticket Tier" icon="tag">
    The tier you purchased (e.g. Early Bird, General Admission, VIP) and any included perks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Attendee Name" icon="user">
    The name registered to this ticket, used for check-in verification.
  </Card>
  <Card title="QR Code" icon="qrcode">
    Your unique QR code that the organiser scans at the entrance to check you in.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Download Your Ticket

To save a copy of your ticket for offline use:

1. Open the ticket detail view in your dashboard.
2. Click **Download Ticket**.
3. HiiFest generates a PDF containing your ticket details and QR code.
4. Save the PDF to your device or print it — either format is accepted at the event entrance.

Downloading your ticket in advance is especially useful in venues with limited mobile connectivity.

## Transfer a Ticket to Someone Else

If you can no longer attend and want to give your ticket to another person:

1. Open the ticket in your dashboard.
2. Click **Transfer Ticket**.
3. Enter the recipient's email address — they must have or create a HiiFest account.
4. Confirm the transfer. The ticket is immediately moved to the recipient's account and removed from yours.

<Info>
  Ticket transfers are only available while the event organiser has enabled transfers for that event. If the **Transfer Ticket** button is not visible, transfers have been disabled by the organiser.
</Info>

## Cancellation Policy

Cancellation terms vary from event to event and are set by the individual event organiser, not by HiiFest.

To review the cancellation policy for a specific event:
1. Open the event detail page at [platform.hiifest.app/events](https://platform.hiifest.app/events).
2. Scroll to the **Cancellation Policy** section near the bottom of the page.
3. Read the terms carefully before completing your purchase.

To request a cancellation or refund after purchase, contact the organiser directly through the event page or via the **Help** button on your ticket detail view.

<Warning>
  HiiFest does not set or enforce cancellation and refund policies. Each event organiser defines their own terms. Review the cancellation policy on the event page **before** buying your ticket — HiiFest cannot guarantee a refund if the organiser's policy does not allow one.
</Warning>

## Networking Hub

Some events on HiiFest include a **Networking Hub** — a feature available on **Growth+ events** that the organiser enables to help attendees connect with each other before, during, and after the event. If your event has this feature, you'll see a **Networking** tab on the event page where you can browse attendee profiles and send connection requests.
