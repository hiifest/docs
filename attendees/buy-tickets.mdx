Buying tickets on HiiFest is designed to be quick, secure, and straightforward. Once you find an event you want to attend, the checkout flow takes just a few minutes. After payment, you receive a confirmation email containing your QR code ticket — everything you need to get through the door.

## Purchase your ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the event">
    Go to [platform.hiifest.app/events](https://platform.hiifest.app/events) and browse or filter the events directory until you find the event you want to attend. Use the format, city, and category filters to narrow your search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the event page">
    Click on the event card to open the full event details page. Here you'll find the event description, schedule, venue or virtual link information, and the available ticket tiers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your ticket tier">
    Review the available ticket tiers — for example, Early Bird, General Admission, or VIP. Each tier shows its price, included benefits, and remaining availability. Click **Select** on the tier that suits you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details and proceed to checkout">
    Fill in your attendee information: full name and email address. If you are purchasing multiple tickets, add the quantity you need. Review your order summary, then click **Proceed to Checkout**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Enter your payment details on the secure checkout page. HiiFest encrypts all payment data and processes transactions through a certified payment gateway, so your information stays protected. Confirm your payment to finalise the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your QR code ticket">
    After successful payment, HiiFest sends a confirmation email to the address you provided. The email contains your order summary and your **QR code ticket**. Present this QR code at the venue entrance — the event organiser scans it to check you in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Available payment methods are displayed on the checkout page and may vary depending on the event organiser's settings. The checkout page always shows the options available for a specific event.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Many events on HiiFest offer **Early Bird** ticket tiers at a discounted price for a limited time. Buy early to lock in the best rate — Early Bird allocations often sell out days or weeks before the event.
</Tip>

## Didn't receive your confirmation email?

If your confirmation email hasn't arrived within a few minutes of payment:

1. Check your **spam or junk folder** — automated emails occasionally land there.
2. Make sure you entered the correct email address at checkout.
3. Log in to your [attendee dashboard](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard) and navigate to **My Tickets** — your ticket appears there as soon as the payment is confirmed, regardless of email delivery.

If the issue persists, contact HiiFest support from within your dashboard.
