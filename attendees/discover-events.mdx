HiiFest brings together thousands of events happening across Indonesia — from live music nights in Jakarta to wellness workshops in Bali. Whether you're hunting for a networking mixer, a technology conference, or a weekend arts festival, the HiiFest events directory makes it easy to browse, filter, and find experiences that match your interests.

## Browse All Events

Head to [platform.hiifest.app/events](https://platform.hiifest.app/events) to see the full events directory. The page loads a real-time feed of upcoming events across Indonesia, displayed as cards with the event name, date, location, and a cover image. Scroll through the listing or jump straight to a filtered view using the options described below.

## Filter Events to Find the Right Fit

HiiFest gives you three layers of filters you can combine to narrow your results quickly.

### Filter by Format

Choose how you want to attend:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="All" icon="globe">
    Show every event regardless of format — the default view.
  </Card>
  <Card title="In-Person" icon="location-dot">
    Events you attend at a physical venue somewhere in Indonesia.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Virtual" icon="video">
    Online events you can join from anywhere with an internet connection.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Filter by City

Select one of the featured cities to show only events in that location:

- **Jakarta** — the capital and Indonesia's largest events hub
- **Bali** — renowned for festivals, wellness retreats, and creative gatherings
- **Surabaya** — a growing scene for business conferences and community events
- **Bandung** — popular for music, arts, and lifestyle experiences

### Filter by Category

Narrow your results to a specific type of event using the category filter:

| Category | What to expect |
|---|---|
| **Music** | Concerts, DJ nights, live performances |
| **Technology** | Conferences, hackathons, product launches |
| **Workshop** | Hands-on skill-building sessions |
| **Networking** | Professional mixers and community meetups |
| **Arts & Culture** | Exhibitions, cultural festivals, theatre |
| **Wellness** | Yoga retreats, meditation, health events |
| **Lifestyle** | Food fairs, fashion shows, pop-up markets |
| **Sports** | Tournaments, fun runs, fitness events |
| **Comedy** | Stand-up nights, improv shows, comedy festivals |
| **Business** | Trade shows, investor meetups, entrepreneurship summits |
| **Education** | Seminars, training programmes, academic symposiums |

<Tip>
  Apply filters from multiple groups at once — for example, set **City → Bali**, **Format → In-Person**, and **Category → Wellness** to find in-person wellness events happening in Bali. Combining filters gives you the most precise results.
</Tip>

## Featured Events on the Homepage

When you first visit [platform.hiifest.app](https://platform.hiifest.app), the homepage highlights a curated selection of **Featured Experiences** — hand-picked events that are trending, newly listed, or promoted by organizers. Check this section regularly to catch high-demand events before tickets sell out.

## Save Events for Later

Spot an event you're interested in but not ready to buy? Click the **Save** (bookmark) icon on any event card to add it to your wishlist. Access your saved events any time from your attendee dashboard under **Saved Events**. You'll be able to review event details and jump straight to the ticketing page when you're ready to purchase.
