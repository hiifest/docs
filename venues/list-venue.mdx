HiiFest is where Indonesia's event organizers go to find and book venues for everything from corporate conferences and product launches to concerts, wedding receptions, and community festivals. Listing your space on HiiFest gives you direct visibility to this organizer community without the cost of traditional sales channels. A well-crafted listing works for you around the clock — organizers can discover your venue, check availability, and send a booking inquiry at any hour.

## Create Your Venue Listing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register as a venue manager">
    Go to [platform.hiifest.app/auth/register](https://platform.hiifest.app/auth/register). When asked to choose your account type, select **"List venue"**. Enter your email address and create a password, then verify your email to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your venue profile">
    Your venue profile is the public-facing identity for your property on HiiFest. Fill in:
    - **Business or property name** — the name organizers will see in search results
    - **Contact details** — primary email, phone number, and city
    - **About your venue** — a brief introduction covering the type of venue, its history, and the kinds of events it is best suited for

    A complete profile signals credibility and helps organizers decide to inquire.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your venue listing">
    From your venue manager dashboard, click **Add Venue Listing**. Fill in the essential listing details:
    - **Venue name** — the specific space name (useful if you manage multiple spaces)
    - **Location** — full address, neighbourhood, and city
    - **Capacity** — minimum and maximum guest capacity
    - **Amenities** — tick all the facilities your venue provides (see the full list below)
    - **Photos** — upload your best images (see tips below)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your availability calendar">
    Open the **Availability** tab and mark the dates your venue is open for bookings. Block out dates that are already reserved or unavailable. Keeping your calendar accurate prevents double-bookings and gives organizers confidence that the dates they see are genuinely available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your listing">
    Review all details, confirm your listing is complete, and click **Publish Listing**. Your venue goes live in the HiiFest directory immediately and becomes discoverable by organizers searching in your city.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to Include in Your Venue Listing

A detailed listing outperforms a sparse one every time. Make sure you cover the following:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Capacity Details" icon="users">
    State the standing capacity, seated (theatre-style) capacity, and banquet capacity separately — organizers plan based on layout, not just a single number.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Amenities" icon="list-check">
    Check all that apply: built-in AV, stage, loading dock, parking, catering kitchen, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, dressing rooms, outdoor area, and accessibility features.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Location Details" icon="map-pin">
    Include the full address, nearby landmarks, public transport options, and parking availability. Organizers share this with their guests.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Photos" icon="image">
    Upload photos of every distinct space — main hall, outdoor area, foyer, kitchen, and restrooms. Include both empty-room and styled/event shots.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

HiiFest supports the following venue types — choose the one that best describes your space:

- **Ballroom** — grand event halls suited for galas, weddings, and large dinners
- **Convention hall** — large multipurpose spaces for conferences and expos
- **Rooftop space** — open-air elevated venues ideal for cocktail evenings and product launches
- **Outdoor area** — gardens, fields, or courtyards suited for festivals and outdoor ceremonies
- **Studio space** — intimate creative spaces for workshops, photo shoots, and small productions

## Manage Your Availability Calendar

Keep your calendar up to date at all times. After publishing your listing:

1. Go to **Dashboard → Availability**.
2. Click any date to toggle it between **Available** and **Blocked**.
3. Use the **Block range** tool to mark out multi-day periods (for setup and teardown days around booked events, for example).

Organizers see your live availability when they view your listing, so accurate calendar management directly reduces time spent declining inquiries for unavailable dates.

## Price Your Venue

Set your pricing in the **Pricing** section of your listing. Consider offering:

- **Hourly rate** — suitable for short-duration events such as workshops or photo shoots
- **Half-day and full-day packages** — the most common pricing model for event venues
- **Weekend or peak-season rates** — reflect higher demand on Fridays, Saturdays, public holidays, and peak seasons like end-of-year
- **Custom quote** — for multi-day events, large productions, or organizers with special requirements

You can update your pricing at any time without republishing your listing.

<Tip>
  Listings with **professional, well-lit photos** of the space — particularly images showing a real event in progress — generate significantly more inquiries than listings with empty-room smartphone shots. Invest in a one-time photography session; the return in bookings will far outweigh the cost.
</Tip>
