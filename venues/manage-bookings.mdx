Once your venue listing is live on HiiFest, organizers will start sending booking inquiries. Responding quickly and clearly is the best way to secure bookings and build a strong reputation on the platform. This guide covers everything from finding new inquiries to confirming a booking and managing your calendar around confirmed events.

## View Incoming Booking Inquiries

All booking requests appear in your venue dashboard:

1. Log in at [platform.hiifest.app](https://platform.hiifest.app).
2. Go to your **Venue Dashboard**.
3. Click **Bookings** in the left sidebar.

The Bookings panel displays all inquiries in chronological order. Each entry shows the organizer's name, the requested event date(s), the type of event, the estimated guest count, and the inquiry timestamp.

## Booking Statuses

Every booking inquiry moves through a clear set of statuses so you always know where each request stands:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Pending" icon="clock">
    A new inquiry has arrived and is waiting for your response. Act on these promptly — organizers often contact multiple venues simultaneously.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Confirmed" icon="circle-check">

  <Card title="Declined" icon="circle-xmark">

## Respond to a Booking Inquiry

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the inquiry">
    In your **Bookings** list, click on a **Pending** inquiry to open the full conversation thread. Read the organizer's message carefully — note the event date, duration, expected guest count, and any special requirements they've outlined.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your availability">
    Before responding, verify your availability calendar for the requested dates. Go to **Dashboard → Availability** to confirm those dates are open and free from conflicts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your response">
    Select one of three response options:

    - **Confirm the booking** — accept the inquiry as proposed. The dates are blocked on your calendar and the inquiry status moves to **Confirmed**.
    - **Request more information** — send a message asking for clarification before committing. Use this when the organizer's request lacks detail about setup requirements, catering, AV needs, or event type.
    - **Decline the inquiry** — let the organizer know you cannot accommodate their request. Always include a brief reason (e.g. unavailable date, event type outside your venue's scope) to maintain a professional record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and send your response">
    Click **Reply** to open the message composer. Write a clear response addressing the organizer's specific request. If you're confirming, include any logistical details they need — access times, setup windows, parking instructions, and your primary contact on the day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the booking">
    When both parties agree on the terms, click **Confirm Booking**. HiiFest logs the agreed details in the booking thread, blocks the dates on your availability calendar, and notifies the organizer that the booking is confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Negotiate Terms with the Organizer

Not every inquiry arrives with perfectly aligned terms. Use the booking thread to work through the details:

- **Date and timing** — agree on the full schedule including load-in, event start/end, and load-out times.
- **Pricing** — if the organizer requests a rate adjustment, respond with a counter-offer and explain what's included.
- **Exclusivity and restrictions** — clarify any in-house supplier requirements, noise curfews, or restrictions on decorations and external catering.
- **Deposit and payment schedule** — outline when the deposit is due and the payment terms for the balance.

Keep all discussions inside the HiiFest booking thread so there is a clear, time-stamped record of every agreement.

## Manage Your Availability Calendar

Keeping your calendar accurate after a confirmed booking prevents double-booking and reduces wasted inquiries.

When you confirm a booking, HiiFest automatically blocks the event date(s). However, you should manually block:

- **Setup days** — the day(s) before the event when the organizer's team is on-site
- **Breakdown days** — the day(s) after the event for pack-down and cleaning
- **Internal maintenance or private events** — any dates your venue is unavailable for other reasons

To update your calendar manually:
1. Go to **Dashboard → Availability**.
2. Click any date or drag to select a range.
3. Choose **Block** to mark it as unavailable or **Unblock** to release it.

## Handle Cancellations

If a confirmed organizer needs to cancel a booking:

1. The organizer initiates the cancellation request from their dashboard.
2. You receive a notification in your **Bookings** panel with the cancellation details.
3. Review the cancellation against your stated cancellation policy.
4. Approve or dispute the cancellation through the booking thread.
5. Once the cancellation is processed, HiiFest automatically restores the affected dates to **Available** on your calendar.

<Warning>
  Set a clear cancellation policy on your venue listing before publishing — include your notice period requirements and any deposit retention terms. Organizers review this policy before making a booking, and it forms part of the binding booking agreement. If you do not have a policy set, disputes become harder to resolve.
</Warning>
