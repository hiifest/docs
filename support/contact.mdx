## Support Level by Plan

The speed and depth of support you receive scales with your plan. All channels are available to all plans — what changes is priority and dedicated access.

| Plan       | Support Level              | What you get                                                                     |
|------------|---------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Starter    | Email support              | Responses via email; access to the ticket portal                                |
| Growth     | Priority support           | Faster initial response times; tickets are handled ahead of standard queue      |
| Pro        | Dedicated account manager  | A named account manager you can contact directly for any platform issue         |
| Enterprise | Dedicated support team     | An assigned team of specialists with SLA-backed response times and escalation   |

To view or upgrade your current plan, visit [Plans Overview](/plans/overview).

## Enterprise Inquiries

If your organization needs dedicated infrastructure, SSO and SCIM, SLA guarantees, custom integrations, API access, or a white-label deployment, the Enterprise team handles these engagements with a custom scoping process.

Start the conversation here:
[**platform.hiifest.app/support?source=pricing&plan=ENTERPRISE**](https://platform.hiifest.app/support?source=pricing&plan=ENTERPRISE)

A specialist will respond to schedule a discovery call and prepare a tailored proposal. See the [Enterprise plan page](/plans/enterprise) for a full overview of what's included.

<Note>
  **For urgent issues**, email [support@hiifest.id](mailto:support@hiifest.id) directly and mark your message as urgent. If you're submitting a support ticket for an active event emergency, set the priority to `URGENT` to ensure it's escalated immediately.
</Note>
