Submitting a support ticket gives you a tracked, documented path to resolution. Every ticket gets a unique ID, a status you can follow in real time, and a response from the HiiFest support team matched to your plan's support level.

## How to Submit a Ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the support portal">
    Go to [platform.hiifest.app/support](https://platform.hiifest.app/support) and sign in with your HiiFest organizer account. You'll land on the ticket submission form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a category">
    Choose the category that best describes your issue:

    | Category    | Use it for                                                       |
    |-------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | `GENERAL`   | General platform questions and how-to requests                   |
    | `BILLING`   | Invoices, plan changes, transaction fees, payment failures       |
    | `TECHNICAL` | Bugs, errors, integrations, and platform performance issues      |
    | `ACCOUNT`   | Login problems, team member access, and account settings         |
    | `EVENT`     | Event setup, ticketing, check-in, and attendee management issues |
    | `VENDOR`    | Vendor directory, collaboration workspace, and payment terms     |

    Selecting the right category routes your ticket to the correct specialist team and reduces resolution time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a priority level">
    Choose the priority that reflects the urgency of your issue:

    | Priority  | When to use                                                               |
    |-----------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | `LOW`     | Non-urgent questions, general feedback, or future-dated concerns          |
    | `NORMAL`  | Everyday issues that don't block your current work                        |
    | `HIGH`    | Issues affecting an active event or imminent deadline                     |
    | `URGENT`  | Active event failures, payment processing down, or data loss risk         |

    <Warning>
      Reserve `URGENT` for situations where an active event or financial transaction is materially impacted. Misclassified urgency may delay responses for users with genuine emergencies.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your subject and description">
    Enter a clear, specific subject line — for example: *"Ticket sales not opening on scheduled date"* rather than *"Event problem."*

    In the description, include:
    - The event name or ID (if applicable)
    - Steps you already took to reproduce or resolve the issue
    - Any error messages or screenshots
    - The impact: how many attendees or team members are affected

    The more context you provide, the faster the team can diagnose and respond.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your ticket">
    Click **Submit Ticket**. HiiFest will immediately confirm your submission on screen and send a confirmation email to the address associated with your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Note your ticket ID">
    Your confirmation includes a unique ticket ID in the format **SUP-XXXX** (for example, `SUP-0042`). Save this ID — you'll use it to track status and reference the issue in any follow-up communication.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tracking Your Ticket Status

After submission, you can check the current state of your ticket at any time from the support portal. Tickets move through the following statuses:

| Status        | What it means                                                              |
|---------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `IN_PROGRESS` | The HiiFest team has picked up your ticket and is actively investigating  |
| `RESOLVED`    | The team has applied a fix or answered your question — review and confirm |
| `CLOSED`      | The ticket is complete and no further action is required                  |

If a ticket is marked `RESOLVED` but your issue persists, reopen it from the portal or reply to the confirmation email and reference your ticket ID.

<Note>
  **Response times vary by plan.** Starter plan tickets are handled by email support. Growth plan tickets receive priority handling with faster initial response times. Pro plan organizers work with a dedicated account manager. Enterprise accounts have a dedicated support team with contractually defined SLA response windows. For critical issues on any plan, use `URGENT` priority and consider emailing [support@hiifest.id](mailto:support@hiifest.id) directly in parallel.
</Note>
