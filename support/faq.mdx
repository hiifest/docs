Got a question before you commit to a plan, or something that came up mid-subscription? Browse the answers below. If you don't find what you need, [submit a support ticket](/support/submit-ticket) or reach the team directly at [support@hiifest.id](mailto:support@hiifest.id).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do you offer a free trial?">
    HiiFest does not use a standalone free trial plan. However, eligible new organizers can access a **first-month Growth offer** when it is enabled — giving you full access to the Growth plan with no payment required today.

    If your account qualifies, you'll see the offer applied automatically at signup. Your subscription starts at Rp 1.490.000/month after the first month. Cancel before the trial period ends and you won't be charged.

    Check the [Growth plan details](/plans/overview) or the [live pricing page](https://platform.hiifest.app/pricing) to see whether the offer is currently active for new organizers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?">
    Yes. You can switch between Starter, Growth, and Pro at any time from your account settings — no lock-in, no penalty. Upgrades take effect immediately, so you gain access to new features right away. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

    If you're moving to Enterprise, [contact the Enterprise team](https://platform.hiifest.app/support?source=pricing&plan=ENTERPRISE) to scope a custom contract before making the switch.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are supported?">
    HiiFest supports standard Indonesian payment methods, including credit card and bank transfer.

    Enterprise customers can arrange custom invoicing and contract billing directly with the HiiFest team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is onboarding included with my plan?">
    Yes. Support and onboarding assistance are included on all plans. What differs is the level of support you receive:

    - **Starter** — Email support to guide you through setup
    - **Growth** — Priority support with faster response times
    - **Pro** — A dedicated account manager who handles onboarding personally
    - **Enterprise** — A dedicated onboarding and support team with a managed go-live process

    No plan leaves you to figure things out alone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens after the Growth first-month offer ends?">
    When your first-month offer period ends, your subscription automatically continues at the standard Growth rate of **Rp 1.490.000/month**. You won't need to re-enter payment details or take any action — billing simply begins at the start of your second month.

    If you decide HiiFest isn't right for you, cancel your subscription **before** the trial period ends and you will not be charged. You can cancel from your account settings at any time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you support hybrid events?">
    Yes. HiiFest supports **hybrid events** — combining in-person and virtual attendance — on all plans, including Starter. You can configure ticket tiers for in-person and online attendees separately, manage both audiences from the same dashboard, and use the analytics tools to track engagement across formats.

    Higher-tier plans add features like Networking Hub and AI insights that are especially valuable for keeping hybrid audiences engaged.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do transaction fees work?">
    HiiFest charges a percentage fee on each ticket sold through the platform. The fee rate depends on your plan:

    | Plan       | Transaction Fee |
    |------------|-----------------|
    | Starter    | 4.0%            |
    | Growth     | 2.5%            |
    | Pro        | 1.5%            |
    | Enterprise | Negotiated      |

    The fee is applied to the ticket sale value and deducted from your revenue payout. A lower fee on a higher plan can offset the plan subscription cost for high-revenue events. See the [revenue calculator on the Compare Plans page](/plans/compare#revenue-calculator) for a worked example.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many team members can I add?">
    The number of team members you can add to your organizer account depends on your plan:

    | Plan       | Team Members |
    |------------|-------------|
    | Starter    | 2           |
    | Growth     | 5           |
    | Pro        | 15          |
    | Enterprise | Unlimited   |

    If you need more team members than your current plan allows, upgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. The increase takes effect immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I switch from monthly to annual billing?">
    Yes. You can switch from monthly to annual billing at any time from your account settings. Switching to annual billing locks in a discounted rate that saves you up to **20%** compared to paying month by month.

    When you switch mid-cycle, HiiFest applies the annual rate from the start of your next billing period. You won't lose any days you've already paid for on your current monthly plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What support do I get with each plan?">
    Every HiiFest plan includes access to the support team, with higher-tier plans receiving faster and more dedicated service:

    | Plan       | Support Level                                                                 |
    |------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | Starter    | **Email support** — submit tickets and receive responses by email            |
    | Growth     | **Priority support** — faster response times and prioritized ticket handling |
    | Pro        | **Dedicated account manager** — a named contact for your account             |
    | Enterprise | **Dedicated support team** — assigned specialists with SLA-backed response   |

    All plans have access to the [support ticket portal](https://platform.hiifest.app/support). For urgent issues on any plan, email [support@hiifest.id](mailto:support@hiifest.id) directly or submit a ticket with `URGENT` priority.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
