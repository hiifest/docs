For large-scale events — concerts, expos, festivals, conferences — helping attendees understand the physical layout of your venue is as important as the event itself. HiiFest's 3D Venue Mapping tool lets you build an interactive, navigable map of your venue directly inside the platform, assign specific seating sections to ticket tiers, and publish the map so attendees can explore the space before they arrive and find their way around on the day.

<Info>
  3D Venue Mapping requires a **Pro plan** (Rp 3.990.000/mo) or higher. If you're on a Starter or Growth plan, upgrade from **Settings → Billing** to access this feature.
</Info>

## Creating a venue map

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Venue Mapper">
    From your event editor, click **Venue** in the left sidebar, then select **Open Venue Mapper**. This launches the full-screen 3D mapping tool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your floor plan">
    Click **Upload Floor Plan** and select an image file (PNG, JPG, or PDF) of your venue's floor plan. HiiFest uses your floor plan as the base layer for the 3D model.

    If you don't have a digital floor plan, use the **Draw Layout** tool to sketch a basic floor plan using the built-in shape editor — draw walls, add rooms, and define zones manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add sections and seats">
    Use the mapper toolbar to define your venue's structure:
    - Click **Add Section** to draw a seating area or standing zone on the floor plan
    - Name each section (e.g., "Tribun A", "VVIP Pit", "General Standing")
    - For seated sections, click **Enable Seating** and set the number of rows and seats per row — HiiFest generates individual seat numbers automatically
    - For standing zones, set a maximum capacity figure instead of individual seats
    - Add non-seating zones such as stage, entrance gates, food court, and toilets using the **Landmark** tool — these appear as labeled icons on the attendee map
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign sections to ticket tiers">
    Navigate to the **Ticket Assignment** panel on the right side of the mapper. Drag each ticket tier onto its corresponding section:
    - Match your "VIP" ticket tier to the VIP seating section
    - Match "General Admission" to the general standing zone
    - Seats in assigned sections are reserved for buyers of the corresponding ticket tier at checkout
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the venue map">
    Click **Preview** to review the map as attendees will see it. Check that all sections are labeled clearly and all zones are visible. When you're satisfied, click **Publish Map**. The interactive map will appear on your event's public page immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assigning seats to ticket tiers

Seat assignment works in two modes — **auto-assign** and **attendee-select**:

- **Auto-assign**: HiiFest automatically allocates a specific seat number to each buyer at checkout, distributing seats evenly across the section. Use this for large venues where managing individual seat selection would be complex.
- **Attendee-select**: Buyers choose their own seat from an interactive seat map during checkout. This is the preferred mode for concerts and performances where seat position matters. Enable it per section by toggling **Allow Seat Selection** in the section settings panel.

You can mix both modes across sections — for example, allow seat selection in VIP rows but auto-assign in the general area.

## How attendees use the venue map

Once published, the venue map is accessible to attendees in three places:

1. **Event page** — a "View Venue Map" button appears on the public event listing, letting potential buyers explore the layout before purchasing.
2. **Ticket confirmation email** — the map link is included automatically in every order confirmation email HiiFest sends.
3. **HiiFest mobile app** — attendees who have the app can open the venue map with their GPS location overlaid, making real-time navigation on event day easy.

Attendees with assigned seats see their seat highlighted on the map automatically when they log in or scan their QR code at entry.

<Tip>
  Add detailed landmark icons (entrances, toilets, first aid, food stalls) before publishing — attendees rely heavily on these during large-scale events. A well-labeled map reduces queuing confusion and improves the overall attendee experience.
</Tip>
