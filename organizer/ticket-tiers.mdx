Offering multiple ticket tiers lets you serve different segments of your audience — early supporters, general attendees, and premium guests — while giving you precise control over revenue, capacity, and urgency. A well-structured tier strategy can meaningfully increase both attendance and average ticket value.

## Creating a ticket tier

You can add ticket tiers during event creation or at any time after publishing by returning to your event in the [organizer dashboard](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Ticket Tiers section">
    Inside your event setup or event settings page, scroll to the **Ticket Tiers** section and click **Add Ticket Tier**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name your tier">
    Enter a clear, descriptive name that attendees will see on the event page. Examples: *Early Bird*, *General Admission*, *VIP*, *Online Pass*, *Group of 5*.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the price">
    Enter the ticket price in **IDR (Indonesian Rupiah)**. To offer a free ticket, enter `0`. HiiFest displays prices formatted as *Rp X.XXX.XXX* on all attendee-facing pages.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Define the capacity">
    Set the maximum number of tickets available at this tier. When the limit is reached, HiiFest automatically marks the tier as **Sold Out** and stops accepting purchases for it. Leave the field blank only if you want to set no cap (not recommended for physical venues).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write a tier description">
    Add a short description explaining what this tier includes — for example, perks, seating area, or access level. This text appears beneath the tier name on your event page and helps attendees choose confidently.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set availability dates">
    Optionally define when this tier goes on sale and when it expires:

    - **Sale start date** — the tier becomes purchasable at this date and time.
    - **Sale end date** — the tier stops being purchasable at this date and time (even if capacity remains).

    This is how early bird discounts work: set an end date in the future, and the tier automatically closes when that date passes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the tier">
    Click **Save Tier**. The new tier appears on your event page immediately. Repeat these steps for each additional tier you want to offer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tier types and best practices

Use a combination of the following tier archetypes to create a compelling ticket offering:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Early Bird" icon="clock">
    Reward your most eager supporters with a discounted price. Set a capacity limit (e.g. first 100 tickets) or a hard expiry date. Early bird tiers build momentum and generate early cash flow.
  </Card>
  <Card title="General Admission" icon="ticket">
    Your standard tier for the majority of attendees. Price it at your target rate and set a capacity that matches your venue or virtual platform limit.
  </Card>
  <Card title="VIP" icon="star">
    Offer premium access — reserved seating, backstage passes, exclusive workshops, or meet-and-greet opportunities. Price this tier higher and keep capacity low to maintain exclusivity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Free" icon="gift">
    Set the price to Rp 0 for free admission. Use this for open-door community events, press passes, or speaker complimentary tickets. Capacity limits still apply.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Setting capacity per tier

Every tier has its own independent capacity. When one tier sells out, other tiers continue selling. This allows you to:

- Close early bird pricing automatically once the first batch is gone.
- Keep VIP truly limited without affecting general admission.
- Run simultaneous in-person and virtual tiers for [hybrid events](/organizer/hybrid-events) with separate caps for each format.

<Note>
  The total of all your per-tier capacities should not exceed the real capacity of your venue or virtual platform. HiiFest does not automatically validate the sum against an event-wide cap — you are responsible for setting each tier's limit correctly.
</Note>

## Date-based availability

Use sale start and end dates to automate tier transitions without any manual intervention:

| Scenario | Configuration |
|---|---|
| Early bird that expires | Set a sale end date two weeks before the event. |
| Tier that opens later | Set a sale start date to the day you want it to become available. |
| On-door tickets only | Set a sale start date to the morning of the event. |
| Pre-sale for members | Set a short window before general sales open. |

HiiFest evaluates availability windows in **WIB (UTC+7)** by default. You can change the timezone per event in your event settings.

## Transaction fees

HiiFest deducts a transaction fee from each paid ticket sale. The fee rate depends on your plan:

| Plan | Transaction fee |
|---|---|
| Starter | 4% per transaction |
| Growth | 2.5% per transaction |
| Pro | 1.5% per transaction |
| Enterprise | Negotiated individually |

<Tip>
  If you are running a high-volume or high-revenue event, upgrading to Growth or Pro can meaningfully reduce your cost per ticket sold. Use the fee difference to justify the plan cost before your event launches.
</Tip>

Fees are automatically deducted at the time of payout. The amount shown to buyers on the ticket purchase page reflects the face-value price you set — fees are not added on top for buyers unless you choose to pass them on in your pricing.
