The most valuable thing many attendees take away from an event is not the content — it is the people they meet. The HiiFest Networking Hub gives your attendees a structured way to find, connect with, and message each other before the doors even open, continuing those conversations long after the event ends. As an organizer, you control whether it is enabled, and you can shape the experience to match your event's purpose.

<Info>
  The Networking Hub requires the **Growth plan or higher**. If you are on the Starter plan, [upgrade your plan](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard) to unlock this feature for your events.
</Info>

## What the Networking Hub is

The Networking Hub is an attendee-facing feature that appears inside the HiiFest event page. It gives each registered attendee a public profile (within the event) and allows them to:

- Browse other attendees' profiles and interests
- Send and accept connection requests
- Message connected attendees directly
- See who else will be at the event before it starts
- Continue networking after the event in a persistent thread

For organizers, the Networking Hub is a per-event toggle — you enable or disable it independently for each event you create. When enabled, it is available to attendees as soon as they complete their ticket purchase.

## Enabling Networking Hub for an event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your event settings">
    Go to your [organizer dashboard](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard), find your event, and click **Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Features">
    In the left sidebar of your event settings, click **Features**. You will see a list of optional features you can toggle on or off for this specific event.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable the Networking Hub">
    Find **Networking Hub** in the features list and switch the toggle to **On**. A configuration panel expands below the toggle.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Networking Hub settings">
    Customise how the Networking Hub behaves for your event:

    - **Attendee profile visibility** — choose whether all attendees can see each other's profiles (*Open*) or only mutually connected attendees (*Connected only*).
    - **Messaging** — allow direct messages between all attendees or restrict to connected attendees only.
    - **Networking opens** — set when the hub becomes accessible. Options: *Immediately after ticket purchase*, *1 week before event*, *Day of event*.
    - **Networking closes** — set when the hub stops accepting new connections. Options: *Day of event*, *1 week after event*, *1 month after event*, *Never*.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Click **Save Changes**. If your event is already published, the Networking Hub activates immediately based on the opening schedule you configured. Attendees who have already purchased tickets receive an email notification that networking is now available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How attendees use the Networking Hub

Once enabled, attendees access the Networking Hub through their ticket confirmation page or the event page on HiiFest:

1. **Complete their profile** — attendees add a short bio, job title, and interest tags relevant to the event topic.
2. **Discover other attendees** — a browsable attendee directory lets them filter by interest, company, or role.
3. **Send connection requests** — attendees request to connect; the recipient accepts or declines.
4. **Message connections** — once connected, attendees can exchange direct messages inside the HiiFest platform.
5. **Meet in person (at in-person or hybrid events)** — the event page displays a "Going to this event" badge for attendees who are attending in person, helping people plan to meet on the day.

### What attendees see on their profile

| Field | Visibility |
|---|---|
| Name | Always public within the event |
| Profile photo | Always public within the event |
| Bio and interests | Public (configurable per attendee) |
| Job title and company | Public (configurable per attendee) |
| Email address | Never shown to other attendees |
| Ticket tier | Never shown to other attendees |

## Best practices for maximising networking

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Open early" icon="calendar-check">
    Enable networking 1–2 weeks before your event. Attendees who connect beforehand are more likely to seek each other out on the day, leading to richer conversations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Promote it in your comms" icon="megaphone">
    Mention the Networking Hub in your pre-event announcement emails. Tell attendees specifically that they can meet peers before the event — this increases profile completion rates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep it open after" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Extend the networking window for 2–4 weeks post-event. Attendees often want to follow up on conversations that started on the day but did not have time to finish.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Suggest interest tags" icon="tags">
    In your event description, list 3–5 recommended interest tags (e.g. #ProductDesign, #VC, #SaaS). When attendees use consistent tags, the discovery experience becomes significantly more relevant.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  For conferences and professional events, the Networking Hub is one of the most effective drivers of attendee satisfaction scores. Attendees who make at least one connection during your event are substantially more likely to return for your next event.
</Tip>
