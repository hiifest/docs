Creating an event on HiiFest is designed to be fast. From the moment you click **Create Event**, the platform guides you through every required field and gets your event page live in under 2 minutes — no technical setup required.

<Tip>
  Keep your event name, a short description, and your ticket pricing ready before you start. Having these on hand means you can complete the entire setup without stopping.
</Tip>

## Create your event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Create Event form">
    Go to your [organizer dashboard](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard) and click **Create Event** in the top-right corner. A guided setup form opens immediately.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in your event details">
    Enter the core information about your event:

    - **Event name** — choose a clear, descriptive title that attendees will recognise.
    - **Description** — explain what the event is, who it is for, and what attendees can expect.
    - **Category** — select the category that best fits your event (e.g. Conference, Concert, Workshop, Exhibition).
    - **Date and time** — set the start and end date and time, including the timezone.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your event format">
    Select one of three formats:

    | Format | Description |
    |---|---|
    | **In-Person** | Attendees join at a physical venue. |
    | **Virtual** | Attendees join online via a streaming or video link. |
    | **Hybrid** | Attendees can join either in person or online simultaneously. |

    Your choice here determines which subsequent fields appear, such as venue address or virtual stream URL.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the location">
    If you selected **In-Person** or **Hybrid**, enter your venue details:

    - Venue name
    - Full address (street, city, province, postal code)
    - Optional: embed a map pin so attendees can navigate easily

    For **Virtual** or **Hybrid** events, also paste in your stream or meeting URL.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add ticket tiers">
    Create at least one ticket tier for your event. Click **Add Ticket Tier** and configure:

    - **Tier name** — for example, *Early Bird*, *General Admission*, or *VIP*.
    - **Price** — enter the price in IDR, or set to Rp 0 for a free ticket.
    - **Capacity** — limit how many tickets are available at this tier.
    - **Availability window** — optionally set start and end dates for sales at this tier.

    Add as many tiers as your event requires. See [Ticket Tiers](/organizer/ticket-tiers) for a full guide.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure additional settings">
    Before publishing, review the optional settings:

    - **Team access** — invite team members and assign roles (e.g. check-in staff, co-organiser).
    - **Custom branding** — upload your logo and set brand colours to make the event page look on-brand. Available on **Growth and above**. See [Custom Branding](/organizer/custom-branding).
    - **Networking Hub** — enable attendee-to-attendee networking before, during, and after your event. Available on **Growth and above**. See [Networking Hub](/organizer/networking-hub).
    - **Referral program** — activate a referral program to grow your audience organically. Available on **Growth and above**. See [Referral System](/organizer/referral-system).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview and publish">
    Click **Preview** to see exactly how your event page will appear to attendees. Once you are satisfied:

    1. Click **Publish Event**.
    2. Your event page goes live instantly and is shareable via a unique HiiFest URL.
    3. Ticket sales open immediately based on the availability windows you set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you publish

Once your event is live, you can return to the dashboard at any time to edit details, adjust ticket tiers, manage attendees, and track sales. Changes to non-critical fields (description, images, streaming links) take effect immediately without unpublishing.

<Note>
  The number of events you can run per month depends on your plan:

  | Plan | Events per month | Max attendees per event |
  |---|---|---|
  | Starter | 5 | 150 |
  | Growth | 20 | 500 |
  | Pro | Unlimited | Unlimited |
  | Enterprise | Unlimited | Unlimited |

  If you reach your monthly event limit, new event creation is paused until the next billing cycle or you upgrade your plan.
</Note>
