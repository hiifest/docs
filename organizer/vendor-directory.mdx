## Searching and filtering vendors

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Vendor Directory">
    From your dashboard, click **Vendors** in the left navigation sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for vendors">
    Use the **Search** bar to find vendors by name, keyword, or service type (e.g., "sound system Jakarta").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Narrow your results using the available filters:
    - **Category** — select one or more service categories (AV, catering, photography, etc.)
    - **City** — filter by vendor base city or service coverage area
    - **Price range** — set a minimum and maximum budget range
    - **Rating** — filter by minimum star rating from past organizer reviews
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the results">
    Review the results grid. Each vendor card shows their name, category, city, starting price, and average rating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a vendor profile">
    Click any vendor card to view their full profile, including portfolio, service packages, reviews from other HiiFest organizers, and typical response time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sending an inquiry to a vendor

Once you've found a vendor you're interested in, send them an inquiry directly through HiiFest — no need to find their number or email separately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the vendor's profile">
    Click the vendor card from your search results to open their full profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Send Inquiry'">
    Click the **Send Inquiry** button on the right side of their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your event details">
    Complete the inquiry form with your event date, venue, expected attendance, and a brief description of what you need. The more detail you provide, the faster and more accurate the vendor's quote will be.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and wait for a response">
    Click **Send**. The vendor receives your inquiry via HiiFest and in their registered email. Most vendors respond within 24–48 hours. You'll receive a notification in your dashboard and by email when they reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and compare quotes">
    All vendor responses appear in **Vendors → Inquiries**. Compare quotes side by side and follow up with questions directly in the thread before making your decision.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Vendor collaboration workspace (Pro+)

On the **Pro plan**, accepted vendor relationships unlock a **Collaboration Workspace** — a shared project space inside HiiFest where you and your vendor work together in one place.

The workspace includes:
- **Messaging** — a dedicated thread for each vendor, separate from the initial inquiry
- **File sharing** — upload and share technical riders, floor plans, brand assets, run-of-show documents, and contracts
- **Task tracking** — create and assign tasks with due dates to keep deliverables on schedule
- **Shared timeline** — a visual project timeline both you and the vendor can view and update

The Collaboration Workspace eliminates back-and-forth across different messaging apps and keeps a complete record of every decision made with each vendor.

## Vendor payment terms (Pro+)

Also on the **Pro plan**, you can set up formal **payment milestone agreements** with your vendors directly inside HiiFest.

Instead of a single lump-sum payment, you define a schedule — for example, 30% deposit upon booking, 40% two weeks before the event, and 30% on event day. HiiFest tracks each milestone, sends automated reminders to both parties when a payment is due, and logs every transaction for your records.

This structure protects both you and the vendor: vendors have assurance of payment timing, and you maintain cash flow control up to the event date.

<Tip>
  Use payment milestones together with the Collaboration Workspace to keep vendor relationships fully documented inside HiiFest — a single source of truth from first inquiry to final payment.
</Tip>
