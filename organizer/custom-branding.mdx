Your event page is often the first impression a potential attendee has of your brand. HiiFest's Custom Branding tools let you move beyond the default template and create a page that feels unmistakably yours — your logo, your colours, your visual identity. When your event page looks on-brand, it signals professionalism, builds trust, and increases the likelihood that a visitor converts into a ticket buyer.

<Info>
  Custom Branding requires the **Growth plan or higher**. If you are on the Starter plan, [upgrade your plan](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard) to unlock branding customisation for your events.
</Info>

## What you can customise

HiiFest Custom Branding gives you control over the following elements of your event page:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Logo" icon="image">
    Upload your organisation's logo. It appears at the top of your event page and in all system-generated emails sent to attendees (confirmation, reminders, announcements).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Brand colours" icon="palette">
    Set a primary colour and an accent colour. HiiFest applies these to buttons, links, section headings, and interactive elements across your event page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cover image or banner" icon="photo-film">
    Upload a high-resolution banner image that anchors the top of your event page. Recommended size: 1920 × 640 px, JPG or PNG, under 5 MB.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Event page style" icon="brush">
    Choose from available page layout themes — for example, a light minimal style or a dark immersive style — and combine it with your brand colours for a cohesive look.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Applying custom branding to an event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your event settings">
    Go to your [organizer dashboard](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard), select the event you want to brand, and click **Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Branding">
    In the left sidebar of your event settings, click **Branding**. The branding configuration panel opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload your logo">
    Click **Upload Logo** and select your logo file. HiiFest supports PNG (recommended for transparency), JPG, and SVG formats. Ideal dimensions: 300 × 100 px or wider with the same aspect ratio.

    The logo preview updates immediately so you can see how it will appear on your event page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your brand colours">
    Enter your brand's hex colour codes:

    - **Primary colour** — used for buttons (e.g. *Buy Tickets*), key headings, and the navigation bar.
    - **Accent colour** — used for highlights, hover states, and secondary interactive elements.

    If you do not know your exact hex codes, use the built-in colour picker to select visually. HiiFest displays a live preview of how both colours interact on the page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload a cover image or banner">
    Click **Upload Cover Image** and choose your banner file. The image crops to the recommended 1920 × 640 px display area — use the in-browser crop tool to select the best region of your image.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a page style">
    Under **Page Style**, select your preferred layout theme from the available options. Each theme preview shows how your uploaded logo, colours, and banner will render together.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview your branded page">
    Click **Preview Event Page** to open a full-screen preview of your branded event page as attendees will see it. Check the page on both desktop and mobile views using the toggle in the preview toolbar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save changes">
    When you are satisfied with the look, click **Save Branding**. Changes apply instantly to your live event page — no republishing required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Brand consistency best practices

Applying a logo and colours is a start, but true brand consistency requires attention across every attendee touchpoint. Follow these guidelines to ensure your event feels cohesive from discovery through to post-event:

### Visual assets

- **Use the same logo file** that appears on your website and social channels. Inconsistency between your event page logo and your social media profile creates doubt.
- **Maintain sufficient contrast** between your primary colour and white text. HiiFest will warn you if your chosen primary colour fails WCAG AA contrast requirements for accessibility.
- **Choose a banner image that matches your brand tone** — a clean, high-quality image signals professionalism. Avoid stock photos that feel generic.

### Colour usage

| Element | Uses your colour |
|---|---|
| *Buy Tickets* button | Primary colour |
| Ticket tier section headings | Primary colour |
| Links and hover states | Accent colour |
| Progress bars (ticket availability) | Primary colour |
| Notification badges | Accent colour |

### Email consistency

All attendee emails generated by HiiFest (confirmation, reminder, announcement) include your uploaded logo in the header. Ensure your logo file is high-resolution so it renders sharply on retina displays.

<Tip>
  Save your branding configuration as a template by clicking **Save as Default Brand** in the Branding settings. All future events you create will automatically inherit these settings, saving you setup time for every new event.
</Tip>

### Keeping it simple

Resist the urge to use multiple competing colours or overly complex banner imagery. The most effective event pages on HiiFest use one strong hero image, one primary brand colour, and clean typography. Let your event content do the talking.
