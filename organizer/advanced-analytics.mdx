Advanced Analytics goes beyond the numbers you see on the standard dashboard. With it, you can understand *who* is buying your tickets, *when* demand spikes, *where* your marketing efforts are paying off, and *how* different audience segments behave across your events. These insights help you run smarter campaigns, price tickets more confidently, and build the kind of audience that keeps coming back.

<Info>
  Advanced Analytics requires a **Growth plan** (Rp 1.490.000/mo) or higher. Organizers on the Starter plan can upgrade at any time from **Settings → Billing**. Your existing data is retroactively included the moment you upgrade.
</Info>

## What advanced analytics includes

Advanced Analytics adds four major capability areas on top of the standard dashboard:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Audience Insights" icon="users">
    Understand who your attendees are — their demographics, interests, and purchase patterns across all your events.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sales Trend Analysis" icon="chart-line">
    Identify when tickets sell fastest, forecast remaining inventory depletion, and spot day-of-week or time-of-day patterns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Demographic Breakdowns" icon="chart-pie">
    View age range, gender distribution, geographic origin, and device type for your ticket buyers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Campaign Performance" icon="megaphone">
    Measure the direct ROI of your promotional links, discount codes, and referral sources — down to individual campaigns.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How to access advanced analytics

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your dashboard">
    Go to [platform.hiifest.app/dashboard](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard) and sign in to your organizer account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your event">
    Choose the event you want to analyze from your event list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Analytics">
    Click **Analytics** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Advanced tab">
    Click the **Advanced** tab at the top of the Analytics page. This tab is visible only on Growth plans and above.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you see a lock icon next to the **Advanced** tab, your current plan does not include this feature. Click the lock icon to view upgrade options.

## Understanding audience demographics

The **Demographics** panel breaks down your confirmed ticket buyers into visual segments. Use it to answer questions like: Are most of my attendees local or traveling from other cities? What age group am I reaching? Are my paid campaigns reaching the right people?

- **Age range** — displayed as a bar chart with brackets (e.g., 18–24, 25–34, 35–44).
- **Gender distribution** — shown as a percentage breakdown; only collected where attendees have voluntarily provided this during registration.
- **Geographic origin** — a city-level map view showing where attendees are located, based on billing address at time of purchase.
- **Device type** — split between mobile, tablet, and desktop. Useful for optimizing your event page layout and checkout flow.

Click any demographic segment to filter the rest of the dashboard to that group — for example, click the **25–34** age bracket to see their revenue contribution and preferred ticket tier.

## Campaign performance tracking

The **Campaigns** panel tracks every promotional link, discount code, and referral source tied to your event. For each campaign, you can see:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **Impressions** | How many times a campaign link was viewed or clicked |
| **Conversions** | Purchases that originated from that campaign |
| **Conversion rate** | Percentage of clicks that resulted in a completed purchase |
| **Revenue attributed** | Total gross revenue traced back to that campaign |
| **Cost per conversion** | If you've entered ad spend, HiiFest calculates your CPC automatically |

To add a campaign for tracking, go to **Marketing → Campaign Links** inside your event editor and generate a tracked URL. Share that URL in your social posts, email newsletters, or paid ads — HiiFest captures attribution automatically.

<Tip>
  Compare campaign performance across multiple events by switching to **Organizer-level Analytics** from the main dashboard. This view aggregates data across all your events and is especially useful if you run recurring or multi-event series.
</Tip>
