Your HiiFest analytics dashboard gives you a live view of how your event is performing. From the moment you publish your event, you can watch ticket sales roll in, track revenue in real time, and measure how attendees are engaging with your event page — all from a single screen. No spreadsheets, no manual exports required.

## Accessing your analytics dashboard

Log in to your organizer account and navigate to the dashboard at [platform.hiifest.app/dashboard](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard). From the left sidebar, select the event you want to review, then click **Analytics**. Every plan — Starter, Growth, Pro, and Enterprise — includes full access to the analytics dashboard.

## Key metrics

Your dashboard surfaces four core metric categories. Understanding each one helps you make informed decisions about promotions, capacity, and follow-up communications.

**Ticket sales** show the number of tickets sold, broken down by ticket tier (e.g., General Admission, VIP). Use this view to identify which tiers are selling fastest and which may need a promotional push.

**Revenue summary** displays both gross revenue and net revenue after HiiFest transaction fees. For example, an event generating Rp 42.890.000 in gross sales will show the net figure alongside the fee deducted, based on your plan's rate.

**Attendee count** reflects confirmed registrations — attendees who have completed payment and received their ticket. This number updates instantly as purchases are processed.

**Engagement metrics** cover page views, social shares, wishlist adds, and click-through rates on any promotional links you've set up inside HiiFest. High engagement with low conversion often signals a pricing or messaging issue worth investigating.

## Reading the charts and tables

The analytics screen is divided into two areas: a **visual chart panel** at the top and a **detailed data table** below.

- Use the **date range selector** (top right) to filter data by today, last 7 days, last 30 days, or a custom range.
- Hover over any data point in a chart to see the exact value for that moment in time.
- The **ticket breakdown table** lists each ticket tier with columns for units sold, remaining inventory, and revenue contribution.
- Sort any table column by clicking its header — useful for ranking tiers by revenue or identifying slow-moving ticket types.
- Switch between **IDR (Rp)** and a summary view using the currency toggle in the top-right corner of the revenue chart.

## Exporting data

Export your analytics data at any time for reporting, investor updates, or team reviews.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Analytics tab">
    Navigate to your event and click **Analytics** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export">
    Click **Export** in the top-right corner of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your format">
    Select **CSV** for spreadsheet analysis or **PDF** for printable reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a date range">
    Pick the time period you want to export — choose a preset or set a custom start and end date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the file">
    Click **Download**. The file will be ready within seconds and saved to your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Exports include all metrics visible on screen: sales volume, revenue, attendee counts, and engagement data. You can schedule recurring exports from **Settings → Reports** if you need weekly or monthly summaries delivered automatically.
