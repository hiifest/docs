HiiFest handles ticket payments on your behalf and transfers funds to your registered bank account after deducting a platform transaction fee. The Revenue dashboard gives you complete visibility into every rupiah — what came in, what was deducted, what has been paid out, and what is pending. Understanding the difference between gross and net revenue, and knowing when to expect your funds, keeps your event finances predictable.

## Gross vs net revenue

**Gross revenue** is the total amount your attendees paid for tickets before any deductions. If 500 people each buy a Rp 200.000 ticket, your gross revenue is Rp 100.000.000.

**Net revenue** is what you receive after HiiFest's transaction fee is subtracted. The fee rate depends on your plan:

| Plan | Transaction Fee | Monthly Subscription |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 4% | Rp 499.000/mo |
| Growth | 2.5% | Rp 1.490.000/mo |
| Pro | 1.5% | Rp 3.990.000/mo |
| Enterprise | Negotiated | Custom |

Using the same example: on a Starter plan, a Rp 100.000.000 gross event yields a Rp 4.000.000 fee and a net payout of Rp 96.000.000. On a Pro plan, the fee drops to Rp 1.500.000, giving you Rp 98.500.000. For high-volume events, upgrading your plan frequently pays for itself in reduced fees alone.

<Note>
  Transaction fees are calculated on ticket revenue only. Service fees charged to attendees (if enabled) are passed through in full and do not count toward the fee calculation base.
</Note>

## Transaction fee breakdown

To compare fee impact across plans at a glance, use this reference table. Amounts are illustrative, based on Rp 100.000.000 gross ticket revenue:

| Plan | Fee Rate | Fee Amount | Net Payout |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 4% | Rp 4.000.000 | Rp 96.000.000 |
| Growth | 2.5% | Rp 2.500.000 | Rp 97.500.000 |

Enterprise clients work directly with the HiiFest partnerships team to establish a custom rate based on event volume and frequency. Contact [enterprise@hiifest.app](mailto:enterprise@hiifest.app) to discuss pricing.

## Viewing your payout history

Every completed payout appears in the **Revenue → Payout History** tab with a full audit trail.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your dashboard">
    Navigate to [platform.hiifest.app/dashboard](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your event">
    Choose the relevant event from your event list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Revenue">
    Click **Revenue** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Payout History">
    Click the **Payout History** tab to see all completed payouts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each row in the payout history shows the payout date, gross amount collected in that cycle, fee deducted, net amount transferred, and the bank account it was sent to. Click any row to expand a full transaction breakdown, including individual ticket orders that contributed to that payout.

You can export your payout history as a CSV or PDF for accounting purposes by clicking **Export** in the top-right corner of the Payout History tab.

## Instant payouts explained

By default, HiiFest settles payouts on a **T+2 business day** schedule (funds arrive two business days after a sale). If you need funds sooner — for example, to pay vendors or cover venue costs ahead of your event — you can request an **instant payout** at any time.

<Info>
  Instant payouts are available on all paid plans (Growth, Pro, and Enterprise). Funds are typically credited to your bank account within minutes of the request, subject to your bank's processing times.
</Info>

To request an instant payout:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Payout History">
    Go to **Revenue → Payout History** inside your event dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Request Instant Payout">
    Click the **Request Instant Payout** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the transfer amount">
    Type the amount you want to transfer, up to your current available balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your bank account">
    Verify your registered bank account details are correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the transfer">
    Click **Confirm Transfer** to initiate the payout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your available balance reflects all settled sales minus any previous payouts and pending refunds. Funds from ticket sales made in the last 24 hours may not yet be available for instant payout while payment verification completes.

## Revenue calculator tip

Before setting ticket prices or evaluating whether a plan upgrade makes financial sense, use the **Revenue Calculator** on the HiiFest pricing page. Enter your expected ticket volume and price, and the calculator shows projected gross revenue, fee deductions per plan, and your estimated net payout — making it easy to see exactly when upgrading pays off.

Visit the pricing page at [hiifest.app/pricing](https://hiifest.app/pricing) and scroll to the **Revenue Calculator** section.

<Tip>
  If your event is generating more than Rp 200.000.000 in gross revenue, switching from Starter to Pro typically saves more in transaction fees than the plan costs — making it a net-positive investment even for a single large event.
</Tip>
