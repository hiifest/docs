Word-of-mouth is the most trusted form of event promotion — and the HiiFest Referral System lets you systematise it. Instead of relying on attendees to share your event out of goodwill alone, you give them a concrete reason to tell their network: a reward for every friend who buys a ticket through their personal referral link. The result is organic, cost-efficient audience growth driven by people who already believe in your event.

<Info>
  The Referral System requires the **Growth plan or higher**. If you are on the Starter plan, [upgrade your plan](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard) to unlock referral programs for your events.
</Info>

## How the referral system works

When you enable a referral program for an event, HiiFest automatically generates a unique referral link for each registered attendee. Here is the full flow:

1. **Attendee registers** for your event and receives their unique referral link.
2. **Attendee shares the link** with friends via WhatsApp, Instagram, email, or any channel.
3. **Friend clicks the link** and lands on your HiiFest event page with the referral tracked automatically.
4. **Friend purchases a ticket** — the referring attendee's referral count increments.
5. **Reward is issued** to the referrer according to the rules you configured (e.g. discount code, free upgrade, or external reward).

HiiFest tracks every referral end-to-end, from link click through to completed purchase, and surfaces the results in your dashboard in real time.

## Setting up a referral program for your event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your event settings">
    Go to your [organizer dashboard](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard), select your event, and click **Settings**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Growth Tools">
    In the left sidebar, click **Growth Tools**, then select **Referral Program**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable the referral program">
    Toggle the **Referral Program** switch to **On**. The configuration form expands below.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Define the referral reward">
    Choose what referrers receive when a friend successfully purchases a ticket:

    | Reward type | How it works |
    |---|---|
    | **Discount code** | HiiFest generates a unique discount code for the referrer, redeemable on their next ticket purchase. |
    | **Ticket upgrade** | The referrer is automatically upgraded to a higher tier (e.g. General → VIP) once they hit a referral threshold. |
    | **Cash voucher** | Issue a Rp-denominated voucher redeemable on future HiiFest events (subject to your voucher configuration). |
    | **Custom reward** | Describe an external reward (e.g. "Free merch bundle") — HiiFest notifies you when a threshold is reached so you can fulfil it manually. |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the reward threshold">
    Decide how many successful referrals trigger a reward:

    - **Per referral** — the referrer earns a reward for every single successful referral (best for high-volume events).
    - **At a milestone** — the referrer earns a reward after reaching 3, 5, or 10 referrals (best for creating competitive incentives).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write the referral invitation message">
    Customise the default message that appears when attendees share their referral link from the HiiFest attendee page. Keep it short, enthusiastic, and specific to your event.

    Example:
    > "I'm going to [Event Name] on [Date] — use my link to grab your ticket and I'll earn a reward. See you there!"
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and activate">
    Click **Save & Activate**. HiiFest immediately begins generating referral links for all existing registrants and will generate links for new registrants as they purchase. Each registrant can view and copy their link from their ticket confirmation page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tracking referrals and rewards in the dashboard

Once your referral program is live, monitor its performance from **Growth Tools → Referral Program → Analytics**:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **Total referral links generated** | Number of unique referral links distributed to attendees |
| **Total link clicks** | How many times referral links have been opened |
| **Successful referrals** | Clicks that converted to a completed ticket purchase |
| **Conversion rate** | Successful referrals ÷ total link clicks |
| **Top referrers** | Ranked list of attendees by number of successful referrals |
| **Rewards issued** | Total rewards triggered and their status (Pending / Issued) |

### Viewing individual referrer performance

Click any attendee's name in the **Top Referrers** table to see a full breakdown: how many links they sent, who purchased through their link, and which rewards have been issued to them.

### Issuing custom rewards manually

If you configured a **Custom reward**, the dashboard displays a queue of referrers who have hit the threshold and are waiting for fulfilment. Click **Mark as Fulfilled** after you have delivered the reward to keep your records accurate.

## Best practices

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Announce the program at registration" icon="bell">
    Add a note in your post-registration confirmation email explaining the referral program and prompting attendees to share their link immediately. First-day shares drive the most conversions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Make the reward meaningful" icon="gift">
    A 10–15% discount or a clear tier upgrade outperforms vague promises. The easier it is for an attendee to explain the reward to a friend, the more likely they are to share.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Combine with AI recommendations" icon="sparkles">
    Use HiiFest's [AI campaign recommendations](/organizer/ai-features) to identify the optimal timing for a referral push — typically 2 weeks before the early bird window closes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Highlight top referrers publicly" icon="trophy">
    With attendee consent, recognise your top referrers in your pre-event communications or at the event itself. Social recognition often motivates sharing more than the reward alone.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  The highest-converting referral programs on HiiFest are activated within 24 hours of ticket sales opening. Attendees are most enthusiastic about sharing in the immediate window after they purchase — capitalise on that energy.
</Tip>
