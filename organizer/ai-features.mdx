## How to use audience insights

Audience Insights surfaces aggregated data about your current and past attendees to help you understand who you are reaching and where there may be gaps.

1. Open your event in the [organizer dashboard](https://platform.hiifest.app/dashboard).
2. Select the **AI Insights** tab on the left sidebar.
3. Choose **Audience Insights** from the top navigation.

You will see a breakdown including:

- **Age range distribution** of your registered attendees
- **Top cities and regions** where attendees are located
- **Interest categories** inferred from attendee engagement (e.g. Technology, Music, Business)
- **Returning vs. new attendees** — the share of your audience who have attended your previous events on HiiFest
- **Engagement score** — a composite metric reflecting how actively your audience has interacted with your event page and communications

Use this data to refine your messaging. For example, if 60% of your audience is in Jakarta but your event is in Surabaya, invest in travel-focused copy and early bird pricing to reduce friction.

<Tip>
  Audience Insights becomes significantly more accurate after your second event on HiiFest, as the AI has a larger dataset to work from. Publish your first event even if it is small — every data point improves future recommendations.
</Tip>

## AI campaign recommendations

Campaign Recommendations gives you a step-by-step marketing action plan generated specifically for your event.

1. In the **AI Insights** tab, select **Campaign Recommendations**.
2. Review the recommended actions listed in priority order. Each recommendation includes:
   - **What to do** — the specific action (e.g. "Launch an Instagram Story campaign targeting followers in Jakarta aged 22–35")
   - **Why** — the insight behind the recommendation
   - **When** — the suggested timing relative to your event date
   - **Expected impact** — a projected uplift estimate based on comparable events

3. Click **Implement** on any recommendation to open the relevant HiiFest tool or be guided to the appropriate external channel.

### Example recommendations

| Recommendation | Timing | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Email re-engagement to past attendees | 3 weeks before event | Past attendees convert at 3× the rate of cold audiences |
| Early bird announcement on social | As soon as tiers go live | Creates urgency and drives first-week ticket sales |
| Referral program activation | 2 weeks before close of early bird | Amplifies organic reach during peak interest window |

## Marketing suggestions

Marketing Suggestions generates content ideas and channel guidance based on your event category, format, and target audience.

1. In the **AI Insights** tab, select **Marketing Suggestions**.
2. HiiFest generates a list of content suggestions organised by channel — email, social media, WhatsApp, and paid advertising.
3. Click any suggestion to expand it and see a full brief, including example copy, visual guidance, and a recommended call to action.

You can copy suggestion content directly into your marketing tools or request a refined version by using the **Refine** button to give the AI additional context (e.g. "This event is for senior enterprise buyers, not developers").
