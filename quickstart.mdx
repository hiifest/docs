This guide walks you through everything from creating your HiiFest account to publishing your first live event. If you follow each step in order, you'll have a ticketed event page ready to share in under ten minutes.

<Tip>
  HiiFest's **Growth plan** includes a **1-month free trial** — the perfect way to explore advanced features and lower transaction fees before committing. You can upgrade at any time from your dashboard.
</Tip>

## Steps to launch your first event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Navigate to [platform.hiifest.app/auth/register](https://platform.hiifest.app/auth/register) to open the registration page.

    Choose how you want to sign in:

    - **Google or LinkedIn** — click the corresponding button for one-click sign-up using your existing account.
    - **Email and password** — enter your full name, email address, and a secure password, then click **Create account**.

    HiiFest sends a verification email to the address you provided. Open that email and click **Verify my account** before proceeding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your workspace type">
    After verifying your account, HiiFest prompts you to select a workspace type. Pick the one that best matches how you intend to use the platform:

    | Workspace type | Best for |
    |---|---|
    | **Join events** | Attendees who want to browse and buy tickets |
    | **Create events** | Organizers building and selling events |
    | **Offer services** | Vendors listing packages in the directory |
    | **List venue** | Venue managers showcasing their spaces |

    <Note>
      You can add additional workspace types later from your dashboard settings. You don't have to choose just one forever.
    </Note>

    Select **Create events** to follow the organizer path in this quickstart.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete organizer onboarding">
    If you selected **Create events**, HiiFest walks you through a short onboarding flow:

    1. **Organization name** — enter the name of your brand, company, or community.
    2. **Profile photo or logo** — upload an image that appears on your public organizer profile.
    3. **Contact details** — add a phone number and social media links so attendees can reach you.
    4. **Select a plan** — choose Starter (Rp 499.000/mo), Growth (Rp 1.490.000/mo with a 1-month free trial), Pro (Rp 3.990.000/mo), or Enterprise (custom pricing). You can start on Starter and upgrade whenever you're ready.

    Click **Finish setup** to land on your organizer dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your first event">
    From your dashboard, click **+ New Event** in the top navigation bar.

    Fill in the event basics:

    - **Event name** — give your event a clear, compelling title.
    - **Category** — select the most relevant category (music, conference, workshop, etc.).
    - **Date and time** — set the start and end times. Enable **Recurring** if the event repeats.
    - **Location** — enter the venue address or describe the event location.
    - **Event description** — describe what attendees can expect. Use formatting to highlight key details.
    - **Cover image or banner** — upload a high-quality image (recommended: 1920 × 1080 px).

    Click **Save and continue** to move to ticket configuration.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up ticket tiers">
    HiiFest lets you create multiple ticket tiers within a single event. Click **+ Add tier** to configure each one:

    - **Tier name** — for example, Early Bird, General Admission, VIP, or a custom label.
    - **Price** — enter the ticket price in IDR. Set to **0** for free tickets.
    - **Quantity available** — cap the number of tickets in this tier.
    - **Sale window** — optionally set a start and end date for this tier (useful for Early Bird deadlines).
    - **Perks and description** — describe what's included, especially for premium tiers.

    Add as many tiers as your event needs. Drag and drop to reorder them on the public event page.

    <Note>
      Transaction fees are deducted per sale and vary by plan. See the [Plans & Pricing](/plans/overview) page for a full breakdown.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Publish and share your event">
    Before publishing, use the **Preview** button to see exactly what attendees will see on your event page. Confirm that all details, ticket tiers, and imagery look correct.

    When you're ready:

    1. Click **Publish event** — your event page goes live immediately at a unique HiiFest URL.
    2. Copy your event link from the **Share** panel and distribute it across your channels.
    3. Head to the **Analytics** tab to monitor ticket sales and attendee data in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
